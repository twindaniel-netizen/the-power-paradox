Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the website at this address and the internal publishing tool "The Power Paradox Publisher" (a Facebook/Meta app and a YouTube API client used only by the channel's owner).

What we collect

Nothing from visitors. This website has no cookies, no analytics, no forms and no third-party scripts.

The publishing tool is used only by the channel's owner. To publish videos it stores, on the owner's own computer, an access token for the channel's own Facebook Page and YouTube channel. It does not access, store or share any data about other people, page followers or viewers.

How the tokens are used

Only to upload the channel's own videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and read the status of those uploads. Tokens are never sold, shared or transferred.

Third parties

Videos are published on YouTube (Google) and Facebook (Meta), whose own privacy policies apply to anyone watching there.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in Facebook settings (Business integrations) or in the Google account (Third-party connections). See Data deletion.
