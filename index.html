THE POWER PARADOX

Power, accountability, consequences.

The Power Paradox is a video publication that explains decisions by President Trump and his administration, their collisions with courts, institutions and public opinion, and their documented consequences. Every episode is built from primary documents (court orders, filings, official records) and attributed reporting.

Watch on YouTube or follow the Facebook Page.

Publishing tool

"The Power Paradox Publisher" is the channel's own internal tool. It uploads the channel's finished videos to its own YouTube channel and its own Facebook Page. It has no other users and does not collect data from viewers.
