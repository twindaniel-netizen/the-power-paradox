THE POWER PARADOX

Data deletion instructions

Last updated: November 20, 2026

"The Power Paradox Publisher" does not store data about any user other than the channel's owner, and it has no other users.

To remove the tool's access to a Facebook account: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → select "The Power Paradox Publisher" → Remove. The stored page token stops working immediately.

To remove access to a Google/YouTube account: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access → remove the app.

For any other request, message the Facebook Page.
